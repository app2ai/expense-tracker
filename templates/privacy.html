Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us. This page explains what information Spendly collects and how it is used.

We store the account details you provide, such as your email and password, along with the expenses you choose to log.

Your expense data is private to your account and is never sold or shared with third parties.

We use this data only to power features like spending summaries, category breakdowns, and date-range filtering within Spendly.

If you'd like your data removed, you can contact the Spendly team to request account deletion.
